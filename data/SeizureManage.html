Seizure Management
Recovery position
Airway
Oxygen
Suction
IV access

If BSL <5 give 100ml 10% glucose IV

Diazepam
-  0.2mg/kg (~10mg, max 20mg) at a rate of 5mg/min repeated q5-10min, not to exceed 60mg
-  Needs to be followed with phenytoin

Phenytoin
-  15mg/kg (in 100ml NSaline) given over 30mins (no faster than 50mg/min in adults and 1mg/kg/min in children because of the risk of arrhythmias)
-  Not effective in a toxicological induced siezure
-  Maintenance - 300mg daily

If seizures persist whilst awaiting therapeutic response from phenytoin  consider diazepam infusion - 50mg in 500ml NSaline q4-6h

Consider thiamine 50-100mg IV

Phenobarbitone – 20mg/kg at 60mg/min

Antibiotics
-  If there is a history of headache, seizure and reduced consciousness, assume meningitis or encephalitis and administer ceftriaxone
