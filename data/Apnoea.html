Apnoea
1. Airway Control
- Cricoid pressure if risk of aspiration

2.  Ventilate
- saturate above 95% if possible.
- bag mask, 100% O2.

3.  OP tube

4. Failure to Ventilate?
--> Endotracheal intubation / LMA

5.  Unsuccessful?
-> Ventilate OP airway, 100% O2.

6.  Unable to ventilate?
--> Transtracheal ventilation
OR Cricothyroidotomy.
