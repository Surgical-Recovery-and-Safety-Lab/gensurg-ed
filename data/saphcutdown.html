Saphenous Cut-Down Cannulation
1. Sterile technique
- clean area, wear gloves.

2. Local
- aiming 1cm ant to medial malleolus of ankle
- or antecubital fossa.
- don't use saphenous if lower limb/pelvic trauma.

3. 2-3cm Incision
- transverse over saphenous vein.
- expose 2cm length of vein using blunt dissection with haemostat forceps.

4. Ligature
- ligate distal end of vein
- tie, leaving ends long to provide retraction.
Further ligature around prox end of vein, loose throw, provide gentle traction.

5. Cannulate
- Small transverse incision over vein
- insert largest possible bore cannula.
- pass beyond proximal tie
- tie tightly to prevent backflow of blood.

6. Closure
- interrupted sutures if time.
- else throw a clean dressing over it.
