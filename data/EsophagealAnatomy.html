Esophageal Anatomy
Embryology
Cranial portion from pharynx
Distal portion from pregastric foregut
Endoderm --> mucosal epithelium, ducts and glands
Mesoderm --> lamina propria, muscularis mucosa, muscle coat
Branchial arches --> striated muscle
Visceral splanchnic mesoderm --> smooth muscle coat

Gross Anatomy
25 cm long, from 5th vertical vertebra to 11th thoracic vertebra
Not fixed, moves up with swallowing
Anchored superiorly to the cricoid cartilage; pharynx by slips of collagen fibers interspersed with muscle.
- similarly tethered along length to pleura, aorta, LM bronchus, pericardium.
Laterally deviates to the left
= relevant to approach, e.g. esophagostomy, esopagogastrostomy often performed on the L.
From incisor teeth:
~16cm = UES
~40cm = LES
~25cm = LM bronchus and aortic arch anterior
Divided surgically into:
- cervical -> 18cm from teeth; cricopharyngeus to thoracic inlet at level of T1
- upper thoracic -> 24cm; to tracheal bifurcation at inferior margin of T4
- midthoracic -> 32cm; level of T10 (diaphragm opening)
- lower thoracic -> 40cm; to stomach.
