Notes

Chronic catgut
Smooth surface; drawn through tissues smoothly.
Rapidly dissolves in infective environment or near pancreatic duct
Swells slightly when wet, secure knots.
Very rapid absorption
Inflammatory.

Polyglycolic (vicryl, dexon)
Digestion b hydrolysis, so no problem near proteolytic enzymes
Mild inflammatory reaction.
Rougher than catgut, slight sawing effect on tissue.
Have made catgut largely obsolete.

Natural non-absorbables
(Silk and cotton)
Now largely historic.
Inflammatory
Disintegrate over time.
Still popular in US; no evidence and good for handling and knot tying.

Synthetic non-absorbable braided
(e.g. Ethibond, Ticron)
Need 4-5 throws cf 3 for silk

Synthetic nonabsorbable mono
Nylon, prolene.
So slippery that 5+ knots required.
Least reactive of all products
Very popular in vascular surgery.

Steel wire
Now historic, except for median sternotomy wounds.
