Laparoscopic Access
2-3cm infraumbilical vertical incision.
Use metzembaums to define cicatrix.
Grasp with an Allis.
Further define cicatrix with metzembaum down linea alba.
Regrasp, then grasp linea distal.
Horizontal incision through fascia.
Regrasp on edges of incision.
Cut wide enough hole to fit Hassan.
Look carefully through.
Gain access to intra-abdo compartment with a closed clip.
- may have to pick up a non-adherent peritoneum with clips. & cut.
Use a maxon J-stitch to encircle hole.
Insert Hassan.
Tie a half-hitch and throw around Hassan teeth.
Inflate abdo.
Insert ports.
