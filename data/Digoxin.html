Digoxin
Actions
Prolongs AV conduction
Prolongs depolarisation
Vagal effect at SA
Weak inotrope
Can cause peripheral vasoconstriction
Onset is 1-2hrs post dose
Use
AF
- load at 0.25-1.5mg IV or oral if previously untreated
- maintain at 0.125-0.375 per day
Adverse
Enhanced effect in low K+, thyroid and Mg++ states
Do not use in hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, AV block, sick-sinus syndrome.
