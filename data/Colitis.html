Causes

Infective
Bacterial
- campylobacter: self-limiting within a week; or can treat with erythromycin 250mg qid, cipro 250-500mg bd or doxycycline 100 mg bd for 7-10d
- c-diff (see notes
- E coli (toxin-producing virulent strains)
- Salmonella (S. enteritides) - most require no Abx; (S.typhi) - always treat with 3rd gen cephalosporins (be alert for resistance)
- Shigella - usually self-limiting may need a 3rd-gen cephalosporin for 3-5d; may infect GB and shed toxin for life; can cause bleeding or rarely perforation, splenomegaly and systemic disease possible.
- Vibrio Cholerae - supportive Rx +/- azithromycin

Viral
- Rotavirus, Norwalk, CMV (esp immunocompromised)
- Self limiting but anti-virals in immunocompromised (eg gancyclovir)

Parasitic
- Amoebic dysentry - entamoeba histolytica = metronidazole for 10d
- Giardia - metro 5d
- Cryptosporidium - nitaxzoxanide
- Schistosomiais (fluke) = praziquantel

Non infective
IBD
Ischaemic cholitis
Diversion colitis

Miscellaneous
NSAIDS
Amyloidosis
Behcet's
Malacoplakia
Collagenous colitis
