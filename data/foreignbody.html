Intro
Dominant contributors are dentures, alcohol and poor chewing.
Children at risk due to play
- and 90% of FB deaths occur in children <5.
Adult Managment
1.  Recognitise obstruction
- cafe coronary - grips throat.
- cough / insp stridor
--> initially only encourage to spit out foreign body
- partial if high pitched stridor, distress, cyanosis
--> call ambulance
--> high flow O2, monitor sats, give fluids, get CXR, arrange for removal with rigid bronchoscopy.
- complete if cannot speak, breathe or cough.
--> ask if they are choking.
THEN if complete:

2.  5 Back Blows <--> 5 Abdo Thrusts
Back blows
- stand to side and slightly behind
- lean victim well forward
- five sharp blows between shoulder blades with heel of hand.
- if lying, roll to side, support chest with thigh and apply blows.
Abdo Thrusts
Don't do if obese or pregnant.
Never place hands on xiphoid or lower rib margens.
Rather between navel and tip of xiphoid.
--> expect some stomach regurgitation.
- stand behind victim, wrap arms around waist.
- make a fist, thumb side against abdo as above.
- grasp fist with other hand.
- quick upward thrusts.

Continue cycles of back blows / abdo thrusts until -->

3.  Unconscious?
--> Open airway
--> Finger Sweep
- draw tongue away from back of throat, insert index finger, hook.
--> Ventilate (2 attempts)
--> 15 chest compressions

THEN:
--> Laryngoscopy & removal ASAP
OR transtracheal ventilation
OR cricothyroidotomy.
- if obstruction low, these may also fail.
Child Management

1.  Recognise obstruction
- particularly in a healthy child while eating / playing with small toys
- resp distress, cough, gag, stridor.
- if partial, high flow O2, monitor sats, call for specialist, fluids, CXR, arrange rigid bronchoscopy.
- if small FB, lodges in bronchus, hyperepands hemithorax.

2.  5 Back blows <--> 5 Abdo thrusts / Chest thrusts
1-8yrs
Encourage efforts first.
If clearly inneffective perform back blows then abdo thrusts
- hold in prone position across thighs.
- abdo thrusts as for adults
0-1yrs
- hold infant in prone, smart smacks between shoulder blades.
- chest thrusts are similar to infant chest compressions, but slower and sharper.

3.  Unconscious?
--> Open airway
--> Examine & remove FB
--> Attempt ventilate x1
--> 5 Chest compressions

THEN:
--> laryngoscopy + removal ASAP
OR transtracheal ventilation
OR cricothyroidotomy.
- if obstruction low, these may also fail.

THEN: intubate.
