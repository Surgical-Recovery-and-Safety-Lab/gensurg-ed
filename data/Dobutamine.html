Dobutamine
Action
Stimulates B1 receptors
Some effect on B2 receptors
Slight effect on Alpha receptors
--> increases cardiac output
--> reduces SCR
Use
Myocardial failure
- used in CCU for cardiogenic shock.
Adverse
Tachycardia, extrasystoles
Hypotension due to B2 vasodilator effect
- inotrope effect may not compensate for this
Dose
5-10ug/kg IV infusion, peripheral line.
