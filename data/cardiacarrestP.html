Mechanism

Decompensating
- Usual in children
Pre-arrest condition eg hypoxia +/- hypercarbia, SIDS, airway obstruction, sepsis, near-drowning, poisoning, neurological disease.
- less oxygen reserves, used twice as quickly.
--> deterioration is rapid:
 resp arrest --> bradycardia --> asystole
Manage and treat underlying cause, preferably prior to decompensation.

Sudden primary cardiac arrythmia
VT/VF
- uncommon; <10% of kiddie arrests.
- only in preexisting cardiac disease.

Chain of Child Survival

Prevention
Early CPR
Early activation of EMS
Early advanced care

- Call fast rather than call first for defibrillator (unless known cardiac disease pt).
- Rather early ventilation is critical to correct hypoxia.
- CPR favours ventilation.

Management Plan
  • Ensure Safety
  • Check Responsiveness
    • Send for help
  • Open airway
  • Check breathing (<10s)
  • 5 attempted effective breaths
  • Check circulation (<10s)
  • CPR (1 min)
    • Go for help
ASAP:  Attach defibrillator, assess rhythm.
  • VT/VF --> 2, 2, then 4 J/kg
    • Persisting VT/VF --> CPR 1 min, then 4,4,4, repeat until alive or dead.
    • Adrenaline 10mcg/kg every second triplet.
    • Amiodarone 5mg/kg after every 3 sequences (earlier if shock transiently successful)
    • Think about reversible causes
    • If asystole results from shock, CPR 1min then reassess.  No adrenaline else VF/VT again.
    • If sinus results, check pulse and cycle as for asystole below if absent.
  • Asystole
    • CPR, reassess circulation every 3 minutes (<10s).
    • Adrenaline 10mcg/kg after every reassessment
  • Sinus rhythm but no pulse - oh dear, PEA.

Advanced Adjuncts

Intubation
- do not interrupt CPR>30s
- uncuffed <8yrs as risk of pressure necrosis.
- 3-3.5 tube in newborns
- 4 by 6-9mo
- then 4+(age/4) for uncuffed
- and 3+(age/4) for cuffed
- insert to tube size x 3 or if >2yrs to (Age/2)+12

or LMA

IV access
If peripheral venous IV not immediately establish
- go for interosseous cannulation
- rapid, simple and safe, particularly good for <2yrs, but good to 7 yrs.
- perpendicularly into ant surface of tibia, 1-3cm inf to tuberosity.
- should get marrow aspirate back.
Give crystalloid
- 20ml/kg as required
- if haem shock persists at >40-60ml/kg given, give blood.

Drugs
- Follow with 5-10ml saline flush
Adrenaline as above
- can be given down ET tube in 10x IV dose if IV access not obtained.
- if invasive pressure monitoring in, keep diastolic P>20mmHg with adrenaline.
--> by continuous infusion, use 0.1-0.2mcg/kg/min, titrate to effect.
Atropine
- use only if suggestion that vagal tone causes asystole.
- dose of 0.02mg/kg.
- minimum dose 0.1mg else paradoxical brady.
- max of 1mg, 2 in adolescents.
Amiodarone 5mg/kg as above
Consider pacemaker, buffers.
- sodium bicarb only late in intubated hyperventilated victims, adrenalin used and severe metabolic acidosis.
- first dose at 10mins, then 10mins thereafter.
- flush it well - it can inactivate catecholamines otherwise.

CPR Technique

Airway
Open with slight head tilt and chin lift, jaw thrust
- avoid excessive extension in children
- jaw thrust is best
- infants may prefer nose breathing

Assess Breathing
If chest /abdo moving but no breathing, reattempt airway opening
- consider FB obstruction.

Rescue Breathing
Nose or mouth-to-nose-and-mouth in infants
Each breath makes chest rise, each 1-1.5s.
- slow breaths at low pressure.
- breath yourself between each
- if difficult, ensure open airway, treat for FB.
Gastric distension impairs ventilation
- insert a nasogastric tube for decompression
- do not press on stomach.

CPR
Check circulation
<10s to do so.
Look for signs of life, breathing, pulse.
- brachial, axillary or femoral in infants
- carotid in children.

CPR Technique
If circulation absent, or infant pulse <60, begin.
Jx of middle and lower thirds of sternum.
Always 100/min, 5:1 ratio unless larger children.
Depth of 1/3 AP diametre of chest

Infants
Two fingers of one hand
Upper finger one finger breadth below nipple line.
Can encircle chest with bith hands, thumbs over midsternum in neonates.
- more difficult with a single rescuer.
Depth 1-1.5cm

Children
Heel of one hand, one finger breadth above xiphisterhum.
Depth 2-3cm

Larger children
Adult site and compression ratios.
Depth 3-4cm.

Going for Help
If alone, conduct 1min of CPR first.
Carry infants to phone if no trauma.
Resume CPR ASAP.

Defibrillation
Indicated in VF/VT.
Paediatric paddles in kids <10kg.
- if repeatedly unsuccesful, consider changing positions to side to side or front to back.
Avoid AEDs if possible.

Technique
Charge paddles upon placement on chest.
Place just to R of upper sternum, below clavicle, and just to left of apex with gel/pads.
- Remove metal, clear of pacemaker.
Derive ECG rhythm from paddles.
- If not sure of rhythm, check leads are correctly placed.
- Turn gain up - must be >1mV for VF.
- If unsure, shock.
Shock delivering ~8kg weight on chest.
- say 'stand back' & don't touch.

If VF/VT persists
Spend next minute doing CPR then retry.
Place ECG electrodes on chest.
Perform adjunctive manoeuvres.

Other rhythms
Don't shock asystole.
Ventricular standstill (p waves w/out qrs) can be treated with transcutaneous pacing.


--> Adequate breathing / circulation
Recovery position.

--> Apnoea but circulation

Then reassess breathing / circulation.

Then reassess.
