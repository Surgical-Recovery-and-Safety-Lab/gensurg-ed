MANAGEMENT

Acute
Pass a rigid sigmoidosope to the site of the twist
Lubricate a large rectal tube (36F) and pass it into the twist

Operative
Generally only if strangulated.
- needs to twist 180 to obstruct and 360 to strangulate.
Modified lithotomy position.
Sigmoid resection, either with anastomosis or as a Hartmann's procedure.
Sigmoidopexy is a theoretical option of sticking the sigmoid against the lateral abdo wall if too sick for a resection.
