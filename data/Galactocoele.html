Galactocoele
INTRODUCTION
Milk-filled cyst beneath the nipple.
D E A B M I M

EPIDEMIOLOGY
Usually occurs on cessation of lactation or when feeding frequency curtailed significantly.

D E A B M I M
 
AETIOLOGY
Probably inspissated milk within ducts.
D E A B M I M
 
BIOLOGICAL BEHAVIOUR
Natural history
May occur up to 10mo after breast feeding stopped.
D E A B M I M
 
MANIFESTATIONS

Symptoms
Lump
Usually stopped breastfeeding recently.

Signs
Swelling usually in central portion of breast or under the nipple.

D E A B M I M

INVESTIGATIONS

Aspiration
Usually produces thick creamy material.
May be tinged green or brown.
Sterile despite these appearances.
D E A B M I M

MANAGEMENT

Aspirate it.
- this confirms the diagnosis and treats it.
Operation reserved for those that are infected or unable to be aspirated.
D E A B M I M
