Dopamine
Action
Stimulates B1 and dopamine receptors
Dose-dependent stimulation of alpha-receptors
At low dose: increases renal and hepatic bld flow, splanchnic vasodilation
Uses
Shock and hypotension
- first choice inotrope in some ICUs, not commonly at low doses.
Maintains urinary output - controversial use; adequate fluid loading must be without doubt (CVP line)
Adverse
Ischaemic necrosis if given via a peripheral line
- always administer via a central line
Dose
1-5ug/kg/min
Alpha effect is at greater doses.
