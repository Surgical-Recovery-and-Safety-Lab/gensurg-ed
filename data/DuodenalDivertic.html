Duodenal Diverticula
DEFINITION
Pulsion outpouchings.
D E A B M I M

EPIDEMIOLOGY

Incidence
Perhaps 5-10% background incidence
Symptoms uncommon (usually incidental).

Age
Rare before 40 yrs

D E A B M I M
 
AETIOLOGY

Pathogenesis
Divertic.

D E A B M I M
 
BIOLOGICAL BEHAVIOUR

Pathophysiology
Usually on medial aspect of duodenum.
Most solitary, within 2.5cm of AoV.
- not in D1.

Natural history
High incidence of gallstone disease in patients with juxtapapillary diverticula.

Complications
Serious complications are haemorrhage, perforation and biliary obstrction.
Rarely, enteroliths from bile stasis in the divertic.
--> diverticular inflammation.
