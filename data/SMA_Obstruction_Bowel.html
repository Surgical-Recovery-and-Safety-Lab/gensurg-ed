SMA Obstruction of Bowel
DEFINITION

Superior mesenteric artery obstruction of the intestine.
D E A B M I M

EPIDEMIOLOGY

Rare
More common in women.
D E A B M I M
 
AETIOLOGY

Pathogenesis
Compression of SMA on D3 --> obstruciotn
D E A B M I M
 
BIOLOGICAL BEHAVIOUR

Pathophysiology
Most commonly after a period of weight loss, e.g. critical injury.
- acute fat loss drops artery posteriorly
SMA normally leaves aorta at 50-60 degrees
- down to 18 degrees in these patients --> squashed.

D E A B M I M
 
MANIFESTATIONS

Symptoms
Epigastric bloating, crampy pain
Relieved by vomiting.


D E A B M I M

INVESTIGATIONS

Imaging
Upper GI series shows wide duodeum, then a transition point.
Angiography shows an acute angle of SMA vs aorta (<25degrees).

D E A B M I M

MANAGEMENT

Operative
Surgical mobilization of the duodenum, or bypass.
Mobilization of the DJ flexure can release congenital bands if present.
