Rectourethral Fistula
Uncommon complication of prostatic surgery and radiotherapy
Requires surgical repair
Solomon & colleagues in RPA suggested double-breasted flap repair using puborectalis muscle.
- approach like a sphincter repair, identify the puborectalis and flap anteriorly.
Propofol in Colonoscopy
May be associated with a higher perforation rate in therapeutic colonoscopy.  No evidence for this in diagnostic colonoscopy.
Readmission after Colectomy
Rates are still high - ~10%+.
Higher in ERAS programs.
Risk factors include stroke, VTE, deep-space SSI
DM, steroids, cardiopulmonary comorbidities age >80, leak, sepsis, pneumonia, length of stay are other risks.
Renal failure after ileostomy
Post-operative factors convey most risk and attention should be placed there to prevent.
Reducing UTIs
1. Evidence shows remove them if not required; significantly lower UTI rates
2. Placing in sterile technique after prepping and draping also = evidence-based reduction.
