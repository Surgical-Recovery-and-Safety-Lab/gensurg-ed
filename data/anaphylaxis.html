ANAPHYLAXIS
DEFINITION
Overwhelming hypersensitivity response (where the immune hyperreacts to an innocuous piece of the environment), due to an unknown fundamental cause.

AETIOLOGY
Hyperimmune response.
Various allergens, commonly:
- stings
- foods eg nuts, shellfish
- antibiotics, contrast, NSAIDs, anaesthetics esp neuromuscular blockers.

BIOLOGICAL BEHAVIOUR

Pathogenesis
Systemic hypersensitivity response.
Complex immunological and non-immunological mechanisms.
Local and systemic release of histamine and other vasoactive mediators.

Pathophysiology
Terms such as anaphylactoid vs anaphylaxis are used for the spectrum, but this is largely academic.
Histamine leads to:
- dilation of vascular beds (hypotension, erythema)
- increased capillary permeability (angioedema, hypotensive shock)
- bronchial constriction (asthma-like reaction)

Natural History
Hypotension, bronchospasm and upper airway angioemea can cause threat to life.
Deteriorates quickly in cases to PEA/cardiac arrest due to hypoxia / hypovolaemia.

Complications
Meds
Beta-blockers --> high risk of the adrenaline not working
MAOIs --> block monoamine oxidase.
Tricyclics --> increase complications due to adrenaline.
Comorbidities
People with cardiovascular disease or pregnancy are also at extra risk of death.
Asthma --> worse bronchospasm.

MANAGEMENT
 Management Guide
Stop administration of agent
Call for help
Position supine
High flow O2
Exclude life threatening alternative
Assess pulse, BP, breathing
- get sats, ECG monitroing if possible.

If NO PULSE
CPR
Raise legs
Escalating doses adrenaline
- adults 1-2-4mg
- kids 10-100mcg/kg
2 large cannulae
2 bags colloid stat
H1 & H2 antihistamines
- phenergan 25-50mg
- ranitidine
Extended CPR

If Shock/Andioedema/Bronchospasm
Adrenaline
- 0.5mg IM (1:1000)
- kids get 10mcg/kg up to 0.5mg.
Attempt cannulation
Fluids
- keep SBP>90
- 1-3L often needed, if more consider CVP monitoring.
- 400ml for children, 20ml/kg if 1-2yrs.
H1 antihistamine
- phenergan 25-50mg IV adult
- 12.5-25mg 5-10yrs
- 6.25-12.5mg 2-5yrs
- 6.25mg 1-2yrs
Steroid
- hydrocortisone 250mg IV adult
- 100mg 2-10yrs
- 50mg 1-2yrs
If persisting:
--> repeat adrenaline 0.5mg
- ?Hypotension
--> Ranitidine, colloid loading & invasive monitoring.
--> Adrenaline titration: if BP<80, 1mg/min for 3mins, then 2mg/min if still <90, 0.5-1mg increments therafter if dropping again.  Kids: start 1mcg/kg, double after 3mins.
--> Adrenaline infusion may be useful: 1mg (1:1000) added to 500ml 5% dextrose, rate of 0.5-2ml/min.
- ?Bronchospasm
--> Treat as for asthma
- ?Angioedema
--> Nebulised adrenaline 1mg
--> consider ET/surgical airway.

Theory
Priority order:
Adrenaline
Constricts vascular beds, dilates bronchial smooth muscle.
Can provoke arrhythmia / increase myocardial O2 requirements - so only IV if life threatening.
Fluid therapy
Volume loss requires rapid replacement
Antihistamines
H1 considered more important.
H2 when not responsive.
Corticosteroids
Slow acting but should be routine.
