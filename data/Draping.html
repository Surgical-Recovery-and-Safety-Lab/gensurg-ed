Science of Drapes
Notes

Cotton most common: reusable (75x), low cost, breathe easy.
- died to prevent glare from light.
- 'wicking' is a concern: draw bacteria through from skin when wet (especially if non-treated).
- so use 4 layers to prevent fluid seepage through them or underlays.

Paper drapes have gained common use
- 93% more volume of waste
- 3.5x more expensive than cloth
- excellent barriers due to design.

Incise drapes becoming popular (adhesive covering, incised through).
- theory is that adhesive covering prevents bacterial migration from skin edge into wound.
- low porosity makes this unlikely, and the edges often lift up when wet, and bacteria build up in sweat beneath.
- use relates to preference rather than evidence at present.
