AETIOLOGY

MANAGEMENT

Unconscious Pts
ABCs first
Confirm diagnosis.
- ideally with lab measurement.
50ml 20% IV dextrose over 1-3mins.
- do not use 50% dextrose as corrosive if extravasates.
- then a 10% dextrose drip until eating normally.
- if on a long-acting sulphonylurea a 24hr infusion recommended as risk of recurrence.
Glucogaon 1mg IM/SC if IV Rx not easy.
- eg community setting.
- not for sulphonylurea-induced hypos, instead octreotide SC may be value

Conscious Pts
Rapid acting oral carbohydrate.
- 3 vitatabs
- a small glace of juice / soda.
- jelly beans.
10mins later a sandwich.
Observe until risk passed.

Longer term
Resolve cause.
