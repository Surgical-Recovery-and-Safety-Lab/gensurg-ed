Laparotomy

Examining the Small Bowel
Variable length: 3-5m (variable in-vivo)
- proximal 40% = jejunum; distal 60% = ileum.
- jejunum thicker (feels like a double-layer; shirt through jacket sleeve).
Find the duodenal loop
- displace transverse colon upwards.
- trace jejunal coils proximally to the ligament of Treitz.
Pick up small bowel, geed through fingers to ileocaecal valve.
- note diameter, contents, thickness, colour, masses.
- lacteals may appear as white lines across jejunum in the thin.
- note the mesentery & its lymph nodes in thin.
Abnormalities
- diverticula: quite common, often multiple, excise only if symptomatic.
- inflammation: inflamed, thick, marrow, exudate, fat encroachment, lymph nodes up.
- infarction: if in doubt, return to abdomen and view again in 5m; if still in doubt resect.
